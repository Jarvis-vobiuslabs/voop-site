Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

The Short Version

Voop collects zero personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What We Collect

Nothing. Voop stores your habits, streaks, and entries locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework. This data never leaves your phone and is not accessible to us.

Analytics & Tracking

Voop contains no analytics libraries, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or tracking pixels of any kind. We have no idea how many people use the app or what they do in it.

iCloud Sync

Voop does not currently use iCloud. All data is stored in local Core Data only. If you delete the app, your data is permanently deleted.

Third Parties

We do not share data with any third parties because we don't have any data to share. The app makes no network requests of any kind.

Children

Voop is safe for all ages. Because we collect no data whatsoever, there is no risk to children's privacy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at voop.site/privacy. We'll note the date at the top.

Contact

Questions? Email us at labs@vobius.com — we're a small team and actually read every message.
